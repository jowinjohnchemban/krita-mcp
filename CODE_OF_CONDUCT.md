# Code of conduct

This project welcomes everyone: artists, developers, beginners and experts, whatever their background.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Krita, AI or open source.
- Assume good intent, and give and take feedback about the work, not the person.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful to others who read them later.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information, or sexual or violent content aimed at anyone.
- Using the project to create or share content that violates other people's rights or the law.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block
people who repeatedly do. To report a problem, contact a maintainer privately through GitHub (for example
by [reporting the content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
or the user). Reports are handled confidentially.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
